Tech Manèt is an artistic collective exploring the intersection of sound and visual art, creating digital content and immersive audiovisual experiences. Through the interaction of various media, the collective develops projects that experiment with new forms of perception and visual storytelling.
Tech Manèt also produces live event experiences where sound and image engage in a real-time dialogue, transforming spaces into dynamic sensory environments. Projection mapping, live visuals, and sound design form the core of their research and production, driven by an approach that bridges technological experimentation and artistic inquiry.
